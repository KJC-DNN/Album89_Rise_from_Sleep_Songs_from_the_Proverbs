# Album 89 — Rise from Sleep / எழுந்திரு 

Seven bilingual songs. The ant stores in harvest; the sluggard sleeps until poverty walks in armed. The Lord hates six, and seven are an abomination. Father’s command and mother’s teaching stay on the heart and the neck. Desire is not given the strange woman’s beauty. The commandment is kept as the apple of the eye. The road past her door is refused. Wisdom cries at the gate — better than silver, gold, or pearls.

## Short Note:
Seven bilingual Proverbs songs (6:6–8:11). 
Tamil original + English cinematic companion + Instrumental (MP3)
Same method as Album 80 and 87. No SCP overlay.
